Revork ("the app", "we") is a delivery-route and workforce-management tool for
small delivery businesses. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and
how it is handled.

## 1. Who uses Revork

Revork is a business tool. A company ("workspace owner") creates a workspace and
invites employees ("workers"). The workspace owner controls the workspace data;
we process it on their behalf. If your employer gave you access, ask them first
about data they hold — we cannot change it for you.

The app is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 18.

## 2. What we collect

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Name, email address, profile photo** | Signing in with Google, or when the workspace owner adds you | Create your account, show who you are inside the workspace |
| **Phone number** | Added to your worker profile | Contact between dispatcher and driver |
| **Job details** — designation, hourly pay rate, company name, company address, company phone, company email | Entered during setup by the workspace owner | Run payroll summaries and identify the workspace |
| **Precise location (while the app is open)** | When you start a route or capture delivery proof | Suggest a route from where you are, and record where a delivery was completed |
| **Photos** | When you take or pick a delivery-proof photo | Prove a delivery happened |
| **Delivery data** — customer name, address, phone, notes, delivery status | Read from the workspace spreadsheets you connect | Show the stops you have to deliver |
| **App content you create** — routes, check-ins, proof records, settings | While you use the app | Provide the features you asked for |

We do **not** collect: contacts, SMS or call logs, calendar, health data,
microphone audio, advertising identifiers, or browsing history. The app contains
**no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking across other apps or
websites**.

## 3. Where your data is stored

- **On your device.** Delivery lists, routes and pending photos are cached so the
  app works without a signal. Signing out wipes this cache.
- **Your company's own Google account.** Workspace spreadsheets and delivery-proof
  photos live in Google Sheets and Google Drive owned by the workspace. We never
  copy them to our own servers.
- **Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication).** Account records, workspace
  membership and tracking records are stored in Google Cloud. Firebase acts as
  our data processor.

## 4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. Data is
transferred only to the service providers needed to run the app, all of which are
contractually limited to processing it for us:

- **Google LLC** — Firebase (authentication, database), Google Sign-In,
  Google Sheets and Google Drive.
- **Apple / Google app stores** — only if you installed a build through them, and
  only crash/store metadata they collect themselves.

We may disclose data if legally required, or to protect the rights and safety of
users.

## 5. How long we keep it

- Account records stay until the account is deleted.
- Spreadsheet and Drive content stays for as long as the workspace owner keeps it.
- Local caches and uploaded proof photos are deleted on sign-out; local copies of
  uploaded proofs are also removed after the retention period set in workspace
  settings (default 7 days).

## 6. Your choices and rights

- **Permissions** — you can refuse location, camera or photo access. The rest of
  the app still works; only the related feature is unavailable.
- **Delete your account** — in the app: **Profile → Delete account**. This removes
  your Firebase account, your profile record, your tracking record, and local
  data on the device. You can also request deletion by emailing
  [support email]; we respond within 30 days.
- **Access, correction, portability** — much of the data is in your own Google
  Sheets/Drive, where you can view and edit it directly. For anything else, email
  us.
- **Workspace data** belongs to the workspace owner; contact them for changes to
  records they control.

If you are in the EEA/UK you also have the right to object to processing and to
lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

## 7. Security

Data travels over HTTPS/TLS. Your sign-in session is kept in the device's
keychain/keystore rather than in plain storage, and cached workspace data sits in
the app's private storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep
access limited to the people in your workspace.

policy.
