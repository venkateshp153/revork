# Privacy Policy for Revork

**Last updated:** 23 November 2026

Revork is a workforce and delivery management app used by companies and their
employees. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is
handled.

## Data we collect

- **Account data:** name, email address, profile photo, phone number and role.
- **Company data:** company name, address, phone, email and workspace files.
- **Delivery data:** delivery addresses, receiver names and phone numbers,
  order counts, delivery status, notes and delivery proof photos.
- **Location data:** approximate or precise device location while the app is in
  use, used to build routes, find the next stop and record proof check-in.
- **Attendance and payroll data:** clock-in/out times, worked hours and pay
  rates entered by the company.
- **Inventory data:** item names, categories, quantities, low-stock levels and
  notes.

We do not sell personal data.

## How data is used

- To sign users in and connect them to their company workspace.
- To build, optimize and track delivery routes.
- To record deliveries, proof photos and attendance.
- To show company, team and inventory information inside the app.
- To send in-app alerts and notifications.
- To keep the service secure and troubleshoot failures.

## Where data is stored

Company workspace data is stored in the company's Google Sheets and Google
Drive folder. Account and tracking data may be stored in Google Firebase.
Device permissions and local files are used for photos and offline support.

Workspace spreadsheets are private. They are shared only with the Google
accounts the company authorizes; they are not public link-shared.

Google's privacy policy applies to Google services:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Sharing

Data is visible to the company that owns the workspace and to users the company
authorizes. We do not share personal data with advertisers or unrelated third
parties. Service providers such as Google Cloud, Firebase, Google Sheets/Drive,
Google Maps and Google Cloud Vision process data to provide the service.

## Retention and deletion

Company workspace data is retained for as long as the company keeps it. Users
can delete their personal account from **Profile → Privacy & account → Delete my
account**. Account deletion removes the personal profile and sign-in. Company
workspace files belong to the company and are not deleted by an employee account
deletion.

To request deletion of company data, contact the company administrator or
**support@revork.app**.

## Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to a workspace is limited by Google
account permissions and app roles. No method of transmission or storage is
completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect data.

## Children

Revork is a workplace app and is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The updated date is shown at the top of this page.
